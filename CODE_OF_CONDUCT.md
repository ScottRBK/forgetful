# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Forgetful to be a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language
- respecting different views and experiences
- accepting constructive criticism
- focusing on the problem, not the person
- giving focused and evidence-based feedback

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats,
sexualised language or attention, publishing private information without permission, and
other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in Forgetful repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions,
official project channels, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainer at
[scott@scottrbk.dev](mailto:scott@scottrbk.dev).

Do not submit conduct reports through public issues or private vulnerability reports.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove or edit contributions, close discussions, restrict participation,
or temporarily or permanently ban a participant. Actions will be proportionate to the conduct
and applied in good faith.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
